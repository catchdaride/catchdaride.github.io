Flagship Transit Hubs & Interchange Stations

📍 Vancouver Waterfront Station
SkyTrain (Expo & Canada Lines) · SeaBus (North Shore) · West Coast Express · Trolleybuses

Vancouver's historic multimodal hub uniting the Expo Line, Canada Line (YVR Airport direct), SeaBus catamarans across Burrard Inlet, and commuter rail.
📍 Commercial-Broadway Station
SkyTrain Expo Line · SkyTrain Millennium Line · 99 B-Line RapidBus

The busiest transfer station in Metro Vancouver, linking the Expo and Millennium SkyTrain lines with the legendary 99 B-Line corridor to UBC.
📍 Metrotown Station
SkyTrain Expo Line · Burnaby Transit Exchange Buses

Major suburban transit hub directly serving Metropolis at Metrotown and coordinating extensive bus feeder routes throughout Burnaby.
