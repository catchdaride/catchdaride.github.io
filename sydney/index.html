Flagship Transit Hubs & Interchange Stations

📍 Sydney Central Station
Sydney Trains · Sydney Metro · L1/L2/L3 Light Rail · NSW TrainLink

Sydney's primary rail terminus connecting suburban rail lines, the high-speed Sydney Metro, light rail corridors, and regional NSW TrainLink services.
📍 Circular Quay Interchange
Sydney Ferries · Sydney Trains · L2/L3 Light Rail

World-famous harbor gateway connecting Sydney Ferries across Sydney Harbour, the City Circle train loop, and George Street Light Rail.
📍 Parramatta Interchange
Sydney Trains (T1/T2/T5) · Parramatta Light Rail · T-Way Buses

The transit heart of Sydney's Second CBD, linking heavy rail lines, Parramatta Light Rail, and regional T-Way bus rapid transit corridors.
📍 Bondi Junction Interchange
T4 Eastern Suburbs Line · Eastern Beaches Buses

Eastern Suburbs rail terminus connecting T4 rail commuters to high-frequency beach buses serving Bondi Beach, Bronte, and Coogee.
