Interactive public transit catchment explorer for the San Francisco Bay Area. Explore BART regional rail, SFMTA Muni Metro light rail and historic cable cars, Caltrain Peninsula commuter trains, and AC Transit East Bay buses.

Flagship Transit Hubs & Interchange Stations

📍 Powell Street & Market St Station
BART · Muni Metro (J/K/L/M/N/T) · Historic F-Market Streetcar · Powell Cable Cars

San Francisco's premier multimodal downtown spine uniting 5 BART heavy rail lines, all Muni Metro underground light rail routes, historic F-Market streetcars, and the iconic Powell Street cable car turnaround.
📍 Salesforce Transit Center
AC Transit Transbay Express · Muni Buses · Golden Gate Transit · Amtrak Thruway

The Grand Central of the West: modern 5-level regional transit terminal featuring AC Transit Transbay buses crossing the Bay Bridge, SF Muni corridors, and rooftop park.
📍 San Francisco 4th & King Caltrain Station
Caltrain Commuter Rail · Muni Metro T-Third / N-Judah · Muni Buses

Major Peninsula commuter terminus in Mission Bay connecting Caltrain express electric service to Silicon Valley and San Jose with Muni Metro T-Third and N-Judah light rail.
📍 12th St. Oakland City Center Station
BART · AC Transit Tempo BRT (1T) · AC Transit Local & Transbay Buses

The core downtown Oakland multimodal transfer hub connecting BART Yellow, Red, and Orange lines with AC Transit's high-frequency Tempo BRT trunk line.
📍 Downtown Berkeley Station
BART · AC Transit Transbay (F Line) · AC Transit Buses (51B, 6, 18, 52)

Major East Bay university and transit hub connecting UC Berkeley campus, BART Red and Orange lines, and AC Transit Transbay express buses to San Francisco.
