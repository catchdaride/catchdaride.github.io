Flagship Transit Hubs & Interchange Stations

📍 Brisbane Central Station
Queensland Rail · City Centre Buses

Central Brisbane heavy rail terminal serving all commuter rail lines across SEQ including Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, and Airport links.
📍 Roma Street Transit Centre
Queensland Rail · Cross River Rail Hub · Northern Busway · Long Distance Coaches

Major multimodal transit nexus linking Cross River Rail construction, Northern Busway express buses, and regional Queensland coach services.
📍 South Bank Interchange
South East Busway · Citytrain · CityCat Ferries

Cultural precinct transit hub linking the South East Busway corridor with South Bank and South Brisbane heavy rail stations and Brisbane River ferries.
